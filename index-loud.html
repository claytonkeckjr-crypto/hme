Got Questions?
We Got Answers.

Do you provide the hair, or do I bring my own?

Both! We carry premium raw Indian and Burmese bundles in-house, or you're welcome to bring your own. Your stylist will confirm exactly how much length and how many bundles you'll need at your consultation.

How do I book an appointment?

Tap any “Book” button to reserve your seat online in seconds. New here? Start with a free consultation — in person in Charlotte or virtually from anywhere — and we'll map out your look and pricing.

How long will my install or style last?

With proper aftercare, installs typically last 4–8 weeks and a silk press holds 1–2 weeks. Every service includes a personalized aftercare plan so your crown stays flawless for as long as possible.

Is Hair Mafia good for natural and protective styles?

Absolutely. Founder Trophy Santiaga is a trichologist, so every service — from locs to silk press to installs — is built around the long-term health of your hair, scalp and edges.

Do you offer student or subscriber discounts?

Yes! Students save 10% with a valid ID, and you can subscribe & save 15% on eligible hair extensions. Join the Family newsletter for first access to bundle drops and members-only offers.
